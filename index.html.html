🌞 Photosynthesis

Plants use light energy to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water.

⚡ Respiration

Cells break down glucose to release usable energy (ATP).

🔁 The Big Connection

The products of one process are important reactants for the other.

📚 Learn It

🌱 Photosynthesis

Purpose: Capture light energy and store it in glucose (chemical energy).

6CO₂ + 6H₂O + ☀️ → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
Inputs:
☀️ Light energy
💨 Carbon dioxide
💧 Water
Outputs:
🍬 Glucose
💨 Oxygen
Where?
Chloroplasts in plant cells

⚡ Cellular Respiration

Purpose: Break down glucose and transfer its stored energy into ATP that cells can use.

C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + ATP ⚡
Inputs:
🍬 Glucose
💨 Oxygen
Outputs:
💨 Carbon dioxide
💧 Water
⚡ ATP
Where?
Mostly in mitochondria

🔑 Vocabulary

Chloroplast
Organelle where photosynthesis occurs.
Mitochondrion
Organelle where most cellular respiration occurs.
Glucose
A sugar that stores chemical energy.
ATP
A molecule cells use as a readily available energy source.
Reactant
A substance that goes into a chemical reaction.
Product
A substance made by a chemical reaction.

⚖️ Compare the Processes

Photosynthesis 🌱Cellular Respiration ⚡
Main jobStores energy in glucoseReleases usable energy as ATP
ReactantsCO₂ + H₂O + lightGlucose + O₂
ProductsGlucose + O₂CO₂ + H₂O + ATP
Key organelleChloroplastMitochondrion
Who?Plants, algae, and some microorganismsCells of plants, animals, fungi, and many microorganisms

🔄 See the connection

Photosynthesis makes glucose and oxygen. Cellular respiration uses glucose and oxygen. Respiration makes carbon dioxide and water, which photosynthesis can use.

Photosynthesis → 🍬 glucose + O₂ → Respiration → CO₂ + H₂O → Photosynthesis
